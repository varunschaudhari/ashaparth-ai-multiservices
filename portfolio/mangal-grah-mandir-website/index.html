Shri Mangal Grah Mandir: Temple Website

A complete temple website for the renowned Shri Mangal Grah Mandir in Amalner, with live darshan, online abhishek and shanti registration, Razorpay payments and donations.

mangalagrahmandir.in
The challenge

Devotees from across India and abroad wanted to book abhisheks, make donations and watch darshan without travelling to Amalner or calling the temple office. Bookings were handled manually, which made scheduling batches and halls difficult on busy Tuesdays.

Our solution

We built a fast, mobile-friendly website that brings all temple services online. Devotees choose an abhishek or Hawanatmak Shanti, pick a date, batch time and hall, and pay securely online. A live darshan section connects devotees to the temple’s YouTube channel, and a dedicated donation flow supports Shri Mangal Grah Seva Sanstha.

Key features delivered

  • Online registration for Nitya Prabhat, Panchamrit, Special and Independent Special Abhishek
  • Hawanatmak Shanti registration with batch time and hall selection
  • Secure online payments through Razorpay
  • Online donations to Shri Mangal Grah Seva Sanstha
  • Live Darshan linked to the temple’s YouTube channel
  • Abhishek schedule, timings for Tuesdays and other days
  • Accommodation information, gallery and contact details
  • Multi-language support through Google Translate
  • Privacy policy and terms pages for payment compliance

The impact

  • Devotees can book and pay for poojas from anywhere in the world
  • Less phone and counter work for the temple office
  • A single trusted place for timings, schedules and live darshan
