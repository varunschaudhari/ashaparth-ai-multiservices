What is a live darshan service?

Darshan for every devotee, wherever they live

A live darshan service broadcasts your temple over the internet, so devotees who cannot visit can still see the deity and join the aarti on their phone, TV or computer.

It helps elderly devotees, people who are unwell, and families who have moved to other cities or countries stay connected to their temple every day. For the temple, it also means more visibility, wider participation in festivals and an easier way for faraway devotees to offer seva.

We handle the complete setup from our base in Amalner, Jalgaon district, so the trust and priests do not need any technical knowledge. See how it works in our guide to live darshan for temples.

What’s included

  • HD camera installation, placed with the trust’s approval
  • Internet and streaming equipment setup
  • YouTube channel in the temple’s own name
  • Live stream on your website and app
  • Daily aarti and festival broadcasts
  • Stream monitoring and ongoing support
Choose what suits your temple

Three ways to stream

Start small and add more later. We recommend the right option after visiting the temple.

24x7 live darshan

A continuous stream of the deity, so devotees can take darshan at any hour of the day or night.

Aarti-time streaming

The stream runs on the temple’s schedule, covering morning and evening aarti and daily rituals.

Festival & event coverage

Live coverage of festivals, yatras, utsavs and special poojas, for temples not ready for daily streaming.

What your temple needs

Less than most trustees expect

We check all of this during the site visit and tell you exactly what is required before any work starts.

Trust approval

Permission from the trustees and priests for the camera position.

A clear view

A respectful place for the camera, with a good view of the deity.

Stable internet

Broadband is ideal. A reliable 4G or 5G router can also work.

Steady power

A power point nearby. Backup is useful if power cuts are common.

FAQ

Live darshan questions from trustees

How much does a temple live darshan setup cost?

It depends on the number of cameras, the internet available and whether you want 24x7 or scheduled streaming. After a site visit we give the trust a clear written quote.

Can the stream run 24 hours a day?

Yes, if the temple has a stable internet connection and power. Where that is difficult, the stream can run only during aarti or on festival days.

What happens if the internet or power goes off?

The broadcast pauses until the connection returns. We monitor streams and can recommend backup internet or power for temples where this happens often.

Can devotees watch without an app?

Yes. Devotees can watch on YouTube or the temple website on any phone, TV or computer, with no special app needed.

Who owns the channel and its videos?

The temple does. The YouTube channel is created in the temple’s name and stays with the trust.

How long does the setup take?

It depends on the temple and the equipment needed. We confirm a date after the site visit.
