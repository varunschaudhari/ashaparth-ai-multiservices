About AshaParth AI Multiservices

A digital agency from Amalner, Jalgaon, helping businesses and temples use technology to reach more people, work smarter and grow.

Our story

Technology with a personal touch

AshaParth AI Multiservices was founded by Ashish Chaudhari, Vishakha Chaudhari and Priya Chaudhari with a simple belief: every business and every temple, whatever its size or location, deserves technology that truly works for it.

From our base in Amalner, Jalgaon district, we saw how many local businesses, institutions and temples were missing out on the opportunities of the digital world, not because they did not want to grow, but because they lacked a trusted partner who could explain things simply and deliver reliably.

Today we offer a complete range of services: websites, mobile apps, AI and automation, custom software, SEO, social media, paid advertising and branding. Through our temple solutions and the Maharashtra Devasthan Live initiative, we also help temples connect with devotees around the world through live darshan, websites, apps and online donations.

What “multiservices” means for you

Instead of managing separate vendors for your website, app, marketing and design, you get one accountable team that understands your entire digital presence.

  • One point of contact for every project
  • Consistent branding across web, app and social
  • Faster delivery, simpler billing
  • Long-term support as you grow
