7 Signs Your Business Needs a New Website

Is your website helping or hurting your business? Here are seven clear signs it is time for a redesign, and what to do about it.

Team AshaParth AI 20 October 2026 4 min read

Your website works for you 24 hours a day. But an outdated website can quietly turn customers away. Here are seven signs it is time for an upgrade.

1. It does not work well on mobile

Most people visit websites on their phones. If visitors have to pinch and zoom, or buttons are hard to tap, they will leave and visit a competitor.

2. It loads slowly

If a page takes more than a few seconds to appear, many visitors give up. Heavy images, old code and cheap hosting are common causes.

3. You cannot update it yourself

Changing a phone number or adding a new service should not require calling a developer every time. A modern website lets you update content easily.

4. It does not bring enquiries

A website should generate calls, WhatsApp messages and form enquiries. If yours does not, it may be missing clear calls-to-action, trust signals or basic SEO.

5. It does not show up on Google

If customers search for your service in your city and you do not appear, you are losing business. Good structure, page speed and content all affect search rankings.

6. It shows a “Not secure” warning

Browsers warn visitors when a site does not use HTTPS. An SSL certificate is now essential, both for trust and for Google rankings.

7. It no longer reflects your business

If your services, prices, photos or branding have changed, your website should too. An outdated site makes a growing business look smaller than it is.

What next?

A new website does not have to be complicated or expensive. Start with clear goals: more calls, more bookings or more sales. Then build around them. Our website development team can review your current site and suggest the right next step. Request a free quote to get started.
