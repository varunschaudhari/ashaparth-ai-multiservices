Local SEO: How to Get Your Business Found on Google Maps

When people search “near me”, Google Maps decides who gets the call. These practical steps help your business show up in local results.

Team AshaParth AI 5 October 2026 5 min read

When someone searches for “computer repair near me” or “best restaurant in Jalgaon”, Google shows a map with three businesses at the top. Being in that list can bring a steady flow of calls and visits. This is what local SEO is all about.

1. Claim and complete your Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile is your free listing on Google Search and Maps. Make sure it includes your correct business name, address, phone number, working hours, category and a clear description of your services.

2. Add real photos regularly

Photos of your shop, team, products and work help customers trust you. Profiles with fresh photos tend to get more engagement.

3. Collect genuine reviews

Ask happy customers to leave a review, and reply to every review politely, including negative ones. Never buy fake reviews; they break Google’s rules and can harm your listing.

4. Keep your details consistent everywhere

Your business name, address and phone number should be the same on your website, Google profile, Facebook page and directories like Justdial or IndiaMART.

5. Have a fast, mobile-friendly website

Link your website to your profile. Make sure it loads quickly on mobile, clearly mentions your city and area, and has easy call and WhatsApp buttons.

6. Post updates and offers

Google Business Profile lets you publish posts about offers, events and news. Regular posts show Google and customers that your business is active.
