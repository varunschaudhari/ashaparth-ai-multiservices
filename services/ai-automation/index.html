Frequently asked questions

Is AI useful for a small business?

Yes. Even simple automations, like instant WhatsApp replies or automatic follow-ups, can save several hours every week and help you respond to customers faster.

Will the chatbot give wrong answers?

We train chatbots on your approved information, set clear limits on what they answer, and hand over to a human when needed.

Can AI work in Marathi or Hindi?

Yes. Modern AI models understand and respond in many Indian languages including Marathi and Hindi.
