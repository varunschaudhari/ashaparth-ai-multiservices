FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Android, iOS or both?

For most businesses we recommend a cross-platform app (one codebase for both). If you need only Android to start, that keeps the budget lower.

How much does an app cost?

It depends on features, screens and integrations. Share your idea through our quote form and we will send a clear estimate.

Will I own the app and source code?

Yes. After final payment, you own the app, the store listings and the source code.

Do you maintain the app after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance plans covering bug fixes, OS updates, server monitoring and new features.
