FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to build a website?

A standard business website usually takes 2–4 weeks from the time we receive your content. E-commerce stores and custom web apps take longer depending on features.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website we build is fully responsive and tested on mobiles, tablets and desktops.

Do you provide domain and hosting?

Yes. We can register your domain, set up hosting and business email, or work with your existing provider.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Depending on the project we provide a simple admin panel or WordPress dashboard, and we train you to use it.
