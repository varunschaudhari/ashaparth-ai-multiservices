FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What budget do I need for ads?

You can start small and scale based on results. We recommend a budget based on your goals and competition, and the ad spend is paid directly to Google or Meta.

How soon will I get leads?

Paid campaigns can start generating enquiries within days of launch, and performance improves as we optimise.

Is the ad budget included in your fee?

No. Our management fee is separate from the ad budget, which you pay directly to the platform for full transparency.

Which is better, Google or Meta ads?

Google is great when people are actively searching for your service; Meta is great for awareness and offers. Most businesses benefit from a mix.
