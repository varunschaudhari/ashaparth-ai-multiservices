FAQ

Questions from temple trustees

Will live streaming disturb the temple’s rituals?

No. Cameras are placed discreetly and respectfully, at positions approved by the trustees and priests, and we never interfere with rituals or devotees.

What internet connection does the temple need?

A stable broadband connection is ideal; a reliable 4G/5G router can also work. We check connectivity during the site visit and recommend the best option.

Who owns the YouTube channel and receives the donations?

The temple does. The channel is created in the temple’s name, and online donations go directly to the temple trust’s bank account through the payment gateway.

Can the temple website and app be in Marathi?

Yes. Temple websites and apps can be built in Marathi, Hindi and English so every devotee is comfortable.

Do you work with temples outside Jalgaon district?

Yes. We work with temples across Maharashtra and beyond. Register your temple and our team will contact you.

Can we start with just live darshan?

Absolutely. Many temples start with live darshan and add a website, donations or an app later.
